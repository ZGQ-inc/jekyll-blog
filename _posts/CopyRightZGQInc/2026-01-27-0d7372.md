`Spicetify` + `Cloudflare Tunnels` + `Home Assistant` 实现 Spotify 专辑封面颜色控制智能家居氛围灯。
🔗 核心插件依赖：[dynamic-lights-homeassistant](https://github.com/muckelba/dynamic-lights-homeassistant)

**自动化配置 YAML：**
```yaml
alias: Spotify
description: ""
triggers:
  - trigger: webhook
    allowed_methods:
      - POST
      - PUT
    local_only: false
    webhook_id: [网关生成的ID]
conditions:
  - condition: state
    entity_id: [light.你的RGB灯]
    state:
      - "on"
actions:
  - action: light.turn_on
    metadata: {}
    target:
      entity_id: [light.你的RGB灯]
    data:
      rgb_color: "{{trigger.json.rgb}}"
      transition: 1
      brightness_pct: 100
mode: single
```
灯是易来 YLDP005。自从玩了 HA 家里就多出来各种花里胡哨的物联网设备了。

> **轻量化实现探索**：
> [SpotHomeLight (GitHub)](https://github.com/ZGQ-inc/SpotHomeLight)。目前不知道 Spotify 的封面取色是什么方法，还在研究。
